Secretary-General

Ambassador Saira Elian, Secretary-General of the NPRC

Ambassador Saira Elian took office as Secretary-General in March 2025 after a career in regional diplomacy and international cooperation.

Before joining the Partnership, she served in diplomatic assignments and held senior posts in her country's foreign ministry.
