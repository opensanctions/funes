Governance

The Asteria Conservation Union brings together member institutions, expert commissions, a governing Council, and a professional Secretariat. Its World Congress meets every four years to set the Union's programme.

How the Union is governed

President and Council

The Council is the Union's principal governing body between sessions of the World Congress. The President presides over the Council.

World Congress

Member institutions debate policy, approve the programme, amend the statutes, and elect the Council.

Statutes

The statutes define the responsibilities of the Union's governing bodies and its members.

Secretariat

The Secretariat coordinates the Union's programme and supports members and commissions under the direction of the Director-General.
