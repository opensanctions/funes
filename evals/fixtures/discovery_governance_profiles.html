Our governance

Board of Directors

The Equinox Medicines Partnership is governed by a Board of Directors that approves project selection and oversees policy, finance, and ethics. The Board combines partner representatives with additional members selected for their experience.

The EMP Executive Director is a permanent invitee to meetings of the Board.
