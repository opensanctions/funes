Administrative Council

Administrative Council of the ACA

The Administrative Council provides general direction to the Court's work and supervises its administration and budget together with the Secretary-General. It is composed of diplomatic representatives of the contracting parties accredited to the Court's host state.

Acting Presidents of the Administrative Council

The acting presidency rotates among regional groups for a two-year term.

Budget

Contracting parties contribute to the Court's budget. The Secretary-General reports annually to the Administrative Council on activities and expenditure.

Financial and Budget Committees

Independent experts advise the Council on financial supervision, while representatives of contracting parties consider draft budget documents.
